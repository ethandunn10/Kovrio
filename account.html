Checking whether you're logged in...

You don't need an account to use Kovrio. Logging in just saves your progress to your account, so your scores, History and Insights follow you to any device. Whatever you've done in this browser so far gets added to your account when you log in.

Your progress saves to your account automatically after every quiz.

Logging out also clears your progress from this browser, so the next person on a shared computer doesn't see it. It stays saved in your account.

Delete account

This permanently deletes your account and all progress saved to it. It can't be undone.

What Kovrio stores

If you make an account: your email, a scrambled version of your password (Kovrio never sees or stores the real one), and your quiz progress. That's it. It's never sold or shared, and it's only used to save your progress. Deleting your account removes all of it.
